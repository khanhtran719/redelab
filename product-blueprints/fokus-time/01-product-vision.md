# 01 — Product vision

Version 0.2 · 22/11/2026 · **PROPOSED; readiness is recorded in the independent audit**. Only the direction and documentation-only boundary have Founder approval. This file is not implementation authorization.

## Direction and product promise

**APPROVED — DEC-001:** “Fokus Time — giúp sinh viên biến một buổi tập trung thành tiến bộ học tập có thể tự kiểm tra.” Source: Founder message SELECTED PRODUCT DEFINITION & DEV STUDIO HANDOFF, [global DEC-0001](../../portfolio/decision-log.md).

**PROPOSED — DEC-004:** primary user is a university student independently studying a defined exercise/topic with existing legitimate learning material and a way to compare their answer. The user is not required to study on the phone; they can do the actual work on paper or in another tool. Vietnam/Vietnamese is the initial language proposal, not evidence that Vietnam demand has been validated. iOS and Android remain the intended platforms.

**PROPOSED promise:** make one learning goal explicit, let the student do the work, then help them record what they could do, what they could not yet do and a concrete next step after comparison. The app distinguishes a saved session from knowledge verified by an independent assessor. No claim of improved grades, objectively measured attention, mastery, medical benefit or market uniqueness.

## One complete value loop

Prepare one goal and comparison reference → study with the phone put away if useful → make a self-test attempt → compare with the existing reference/rubric → save a self-reported conclusion and the gap/next action → find that conclusion on return.

The original commitment is retained once learning starts. Changing it cannot silently turn the old commitment into a success. A student who cannot assess the result can save an explicitly unassessed conclusion. Leaving or interrupting the app never proves learning happened.

## Outcome and measurement boundaries

| Layer | What the product can establish | What remains a hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Functional correctness | A commitment/result saved, allowed transitions, consistent recovery, honest labels and deletion | Dev Studio must implement/test; none exercised here |
| User-perceived value | Learner reports a useful gap/next step and voluntarily uses the loop again | No observed cohort evidence in this definition session |
| Learning efficacy | A separate appropriately designed comparison could assess delayed learning or effort with a quality floor | App self-checks, session counts and elapsed time do not establish this |
| Business | Payment/distribution/retention could be evaluated separately | No price, paid plan, conversion or target-market advantage approved |

Efficiency with non-inferior learning and improved learning with comparable effort are different research promises. They must be chosen and measured before seeing results; a tiny test finding no significant difference does not establish non-inferiority. The software baseline does not need to implement a learning-assessment research engine.

## Product boundaries

The proposed MVP is a single-person, offline, self-entered learning-session tool. It uses the learner's own exercises and comparison sources; it does not fetch, license, create or grade learning content. No account/cloud sync, AI tutor, automatic grading, flashcard scheduler, social room, parent/teacher monitoring, camera evidence album, blocker or penalty/streak system. **Timer/countdown/alarms are PROPOSED OUT OF SCOPE for this MVP.** A learner may independently use another timer; no integration is implied.

The operational promise is a clear **self-reported conclusion about a particular attempt**, not measured progress. A learner normally expects access to a comparison source, but temporary absence/inadequacy has a legitimate unassessed path, not a product source-finding obligation. An assisted attempt can retain useful gaps while the summary explicitly says “Chưa xác nhận tự làm — đã đối chiếu có trợ giúp” when comparison was actually declared.

Prior Market/Red Team concerns remain: worksheet, Anki, RemNote or an existing study workflow may already be sufficient. That uncertainty informs claims and the MVP boundary; it does not reopen the Founder's selection. The Founder did not formally reject all other directions or approve all previous council recommendations.
