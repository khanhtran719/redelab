## F1 / R1-inventor-01 — objection M-R2-F1

**Strongest objection and impact.** A self-handoff card can be reproduced by an existing task's note or a sticky note left at the work location. M-S2 verifies Focus To-Do tasks, notes, subtasks and reminders; M-S6 verifies Session stating focus and reviewing/reflection. These do not prove interruption recovery equivalence, but they already hold work context. A separate mobile inbox may add retrieval steps or move laptop work context onto the wrong device. If users already retain their next action in the task/document, F1 risks charging for a formatting convention. M5's switching friction then outweighs a small convenience.

**Strongest defense.** The proposed intervention is at the transition *before leaving*, then shows one next action *on return*. That timing is different from a generic end-of-session reflection or all-purpose task list. It can be useful even when an interruption is legitimate. Market has no inspected source proving competitors execute this full transition equally well.

**Missing evidence.** Frequency and cost of recoverable interruptions; how many interruptions permit capture; where the work occurs; current next-action notes; whether cards are reopened without additional reminders; willingness to change a trusted task workflow. F1's measured restart gain could be merely the benefit of writing any note.

**Improvement to the learning question.** Compare the same three-field handoff in an existing note/task location against the proposed mobile interaction, alongside the user's baseline. Include failed captures, forgotten cards and extra retrieval time. Do not remove sudden interruptions from the denominator. No need to build an app to learn whether the mechanism matters.

**Falsifier — HYPOTHESIS test rule, not a result.** Reject the standalone mobile opportunity if the existing-tool handoff achieves comparable net restart improvement with equal or lower burden, or if most relevant interruptions allow no capture. Reject the mechanism more broadly if restart savings do not exceed capture/retrieval time. A win over no note, while tying the existing-note arm, supports a practice/template but not this app's incremental value.

**Before → after.** Generic duplicate concern HIGH → direct outcome equivalence UNKNOWN; standalone substitution risk remains HIGH by reasoning, confidence MEDIUM. Reopen if repeated voluntary use beats the existing location without coaching.

**Question sent for actual response.** Which specific moment makes a separate mobile surface materially better than a note attached to the existing task, and what outcome would make you concede the app should instead be a template?

## F2 / R1-inventor-02 — objection M-R2-F2

**Strongest objection and impact.** The agreement can be enacted through a visible reply time, a normal chat thread and an agreed urgent word/call. This manual substitute is a HYPOTHESIS baseline, not observed prevalence. CIO-S4 documents native DND schedules/allowed people; CIO-S3 native scheduled Focus and temporary breaks. They can cover notification control, while an existing relationship covers negotiation. M-S4's group/chat coordination and M-S6's Slack status are adjacent precedents, not proof of household equivalence. F2 must persuade the interrupted person **and** the interrupter to change behavior. Benefit for A can be a burden or loss of access for B; payment by A does not solve B's cooperation.

**Strongest defense.** Native DND mainly controls A's device. It does not establish that B has agreed a reply window, can queue a nonurgent household request, or trusts that A will answer. The proposed relationship contract could create value beyond silencing. Forest rooms/Focusmate also concern shared work presence rather than routing household requests; do not treat their social features as identical.

**Missing evidence.** Both parties' current expectations, trust and incentive; requests arriving face to face versus through apps; how often A honors the promise; how B handles a failed deadline; intended buyer and independent willingness by B to participate. Native allowed-person settings do not verify an emergency request will reliably arrive through this future product.

**Improvement to the learning question.** Test a paper agreement plus current chat before testing a dedicated queue. Have each party report burden separately and allow withdrawal without an explanation. Retain an agreed existing urgent-contact route during learning. Count displaced arguments, missed requests and delayed urgent responses as costs, not only fewer interruptions to A.

**Falsifier — HYPOTHESIS test rule.** Reject dedicated-app value if paper/current chat sustains the same agreed response pattern and outcomes, or if B participates only while a researcher/A repeatedly prompts them. Reject the mechanism if fewer visible interruptions are purchased with more conflict, missed legitimate needs or slower urgent response. A nominally successful quiet session is not sufficient.

**Before → after.** Social-feature duplication concern HIGH for a generic room → LOW confidence of direct duplication for this exact interpersonal contract; **two-sided adoption/substitution risk HIGH**, commercial value UNKNOWN. This is an analytical revision, not market validation.

**Question sent for actual response.** What benefit does B receive from using the dedicated queue beyond being told to wait, and what evidence would make you abandon the dedicated queue while retaining the underlying agreement?

## F3 / R1-inventor-03 — objection M-R2-F3

**Strongest objection and impact.** “Write a small criterion, do it, attach a result” can be executed on the exercise sheet or existing task. M-S2 verifies tasks/subtasks/notes/reports, M-S6 reflection, and M-S7 visual planning/AI planning. CIO-S7's Tiimo task decomposition is an additional adjacent substitute. None is verified to improve recall, but they make the smallest-step/reflect vocabulary crowded. F3 adds capture and self-judgment; users can choose easier criteria or upload a photo while learning little. An increase in self-certified artifacts would inflate the product's own metric without producing the learner's desired progress.

**Strongest defense.** A criterion committed **before** study and compared to an artifact afterward differs from checking off an unbounded task or rewarding elapsed time. The inventor already calls for an independent recall check. That is the correct defense against merely measuring uploads. I do not claim task apps or Session already provide the full learning mechanism.

**Missing evidence.** Whether students can choose discriminating criteria; who checks correctness without tutoring; which subjects allow comparable artifacts; baseline use of worked exercises/recall; burden of photos; whether the added process is sustained after novelty. No claim here establishes grade improvement or a medically relevant benefit.

**Improvement to the learning question.** Compare against a paper worksheet containing exactly the same precommitment/recall prompts, not only a loose checklist. Use a comparable delayed question and independent scoring, while recording criterion difficulty and time spent choosing/capturing. This separates pedagogy from a paid mobile delivery advantage. Keep camera capture optional where the exercise itself already records the artifact.

**Falsifier — HYPOTHESIS test rule.** Reject the educational mechanism if more “completed evidence” does not improve an independently scored delayed task relative to baseline after accounting for time. Reject standalone app value if the paper/existing-tool prompts produce comparable learning and repeat use with less burden. Increased easy-goal completion without increased learning fails even if retention looks good.

**Before → after.** Generic task/timer overlap HIGH → exact loop equivalence UNKNOWN; standalone capture-wrapper risk HIGH, confidence MEDIUM. Could be valuable as a narrow learning practice; distribution/payment are still UNKNOWN.

**Question sent for actual response.** What is the minimum independent sign of real learning, and what would make you conclude the benefit belongs to a worksheet/practice rather than a dedicated app?
