# Adversarial review

## Purpose and inputs

Produce falsifiable objections that improve the decision. Inputs: current idea revision, strongest supportive case, evidence ledger, actual peer arguments, constraints and unresolved questions. Independence concerns judgment, not ignoring known evidence.

## Procedure

1. Identify assumptions whose failure defeats the opportunity: demand, differentiation, distribution, payment, lawful data access, technology dependencies or operating economics.
2. Run targeted duplication/substitute checks where tools permit. Consider simpler tools, manual processes, existing bundled features and doing nothing.
3. For each major objection, explain the concern and causal failure mechanism, evidence or uncertainty, impact, falsifier and observation that would reduce the concern.
4. Separate evidenced barriers from conditional risks and unanswered questions. Acknowledge contrary evidence; avoid padding or reflexive negativity.
5. Respond to actual peer findings and review real rebuttals using [debate rules](../../../protocols/debate-rules.md). State whether and why your assessment changed; preserve dissent.

## Expected outputs

Prioritized objections, targeted checks and sources, falsifiable validation methods, simpler alternatives, remaining uncertainty and an advisory assessment. No fabricated rebuttals or consensus.

## Evidence requirements

Apply [evidence policy](../../../protocols/evidence-policy.md). Distinguish VERIFIED/INFERENCE/HYPOTHESIS/UNKNOWN, record dates and search limits, and never claim uniqueness from failed searches. An unsupported concern needs an explicit validation method, not assertive wording.

## Failure conditions

Insufficient candidate/evidence packet, unavailable checks or absent real peer response: mark limitations and incomplete review. Objections without a concern and evidence/test need revision. A lack of objections is not validation.

## Boundaries

Advisory only: no security exploit testing, code, prototype, infrastructure, deployment plan, customer outreach or executed market test. Founder alone approves/rejects/selects; SELECT does not authorize development.
