# Idea evaluation

## Purpose and inputs

Help the Founder decide what to investigate or prefer. Inputs: candidate revisions, evidence/claim ledger, real specialist findings and exchanges, constraints and prior Founder decisions. A standalone evaluation must not pretend to be a completed council.

## Procedure

1. Reconcile duplicates, current revisions and Founder restrictions. Identify missing or stale evidence and actual participation limits.
2. Read [the scoring rubric](../../../protocols/scoring-rubric.md). Apply all nine weights totaling 100%; record each criterion's rationale, provenance, confidence and dissent.
3. Keep UNKNOWN values unscored and totals NOT RANKED; retain exploratory concepts. A hypothetical score is a scenario, not verified commercial evidence. Show assessed weight and evidence coverage separately.
4. Compare ranking sensitivity and critical legal/data/dependency blockers. Do not let a high aggregate erase a fatal premise or missing demand evidence.
5. Present 3–5 best opportunities or fewer, including none: actual supporters/opponents and reasoning, differentiated mechanism, evidence strength, risks, unresolved questions and one proposed validation experiment each.
6. For a full council, verify the real critique-and-response and Red Team requirements in [the session protocol](../../../protocols/ideation-session.md). Save using [session records](../../../sessions/README.md); stop at the Founder decision gate.

## Expected outputs

Transparent scorecards and exploratory lane, uncertainty/sensitivity analysis, decision-support portfolio, preserved disagreements and explicit pending Founder gate. Recommendations and human decisions occupy separate fields.

## Evidence requirements

Use [evidence policy](../../../protocols/evidence-policy.md). Support every important comparison with claims and actual agent records; confidence is separate from score and is not a fabricated probability. Unknown supporters/opponents are not invented.

## Failure conditions

Missing evidence, incomplete debate, unavailable subagents or untraceable findings: report partial/blocked status; do not claim a completed council or commercial validation. No strong idea is a valid conclusion.

## Boundaries

Never record a Founder decision without an explicit human instruction. EXPLORE/CHALLENGE/REFINE allow only scoped research; PARK/REJECT/SELECT do not trigger work. Even SELECT does not authorize development. No code, prototype, infrastructure, deployment plan or implementation assignment.
