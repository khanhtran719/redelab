# Idea Evaluation

## Trigger and purpose

Use for CIO synthesis of council output; a standalone rating is not a multi-agent council.

## Inputs

Current concept IDs/revisions, claim ledger, real specialist exchanges, scoring rubric, Founder restrictions.

## Procedure

1. Reconcile duplicates, stale sources and current Founder status; ensure critical objections have actual reply or mark incomplete.
2. Apply the nine weighted [criteria](../../../protocols/scoring-rubric.md) unchanged. UNKNOWN is not zero or five; missing total remains NOT RANKED.
3. Report assessed weight, evidence coverage, confidence, dissent, sensitivity and explicit legal/data blockers separately.
4. Place poorly evidenced but learnable directions in an exploratory lane; separate Research Priority from Commercial Viability.
5. Provide advisory Decision Readiness: READY_TO_COMPARE / RESEARCH_PRIORITY / BLOCKED_BY_DEPENDENCY / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; never replace existing lifecycle/Founder decisions.
6. Present 3–5 or fewer, including zero, with next decision-changing evidence and stop at Founder gate.

## Required output

Auditable scorecards, unranked exploratory candidates, decisions that additional evidence may change, genuine support/dissent and Founder-facing portfolio.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Apply [evidence policy](../../../protocols/evidence-policy.md). Classify material empirical claims VERIFIED / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN; label other behavioral or technical verification dimensions separately. Cite claim/source IDs and dates where material. Use the compact [finding and assignment contract](../../../protocols/agent-contracts.md) when invoked by a council; do not replace actual reasoning with checkboxes.

## Failure conditions

No demand evidence or incomplete peer/Red Team exchanges: do not claim final council validation, commercial ranking or Founder selection.

## Authorization and collaboration

No product code, autonomous research experiments or fake authorization. Apply [session protocol](../../../protocols/ideation-session.md) and [debate rules](../../../protocols/debate-rules.md).
