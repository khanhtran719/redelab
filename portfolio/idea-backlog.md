## Maintenance

The CIO owns this index. Allocate monotonically increasing IDs `IDEA-0001`, `IDEA-0002`, etc.; never recycle an ID. Search existing customer/problem/mechanism records before creating one. Feature variants usually share an ID with a revised concept in the session record. A materially different mechanism may get a linked ID. For merges, preserve the old ID as an alias pointing to the canonical record; do not delete its Founder history or silently transfer a decision to a materially changed idea.

Evidence status is a concise summary with session claim links, such as exploratory, mixed evidence, corroborated within stated scope, or contradicted; it is never an approval. The four claim classes live in the session record, not an unsupported aggregate certainty label.

Research lifecycle is separate from Founder authority: `CAPTURED`, `EXPLORATORY`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `AWAITING_FOUNDER`. Record `BLOCKED_MULTI_AGENT` in the related session when coordination is missing; do not imply a council completed. The latest Founder column is `NONE` until an explicit decision is logged. It may then contain only `EXPLORE`, `CHALLENGE`, `REFINE`, `PARK`, `REJECT`, `SELECT` and the log ID. PARK/REJECT suspend active investigation until the Founder directs otherwise; SELECT records preference only. These combined columns express the current lifecycle without inventing Founder approval.

Use dates `YYYY-MM-DD` in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh. Link evidence and detailed versions to sessions rather than copying whole research reports here. New evidence can update research status, not a Founder decision. The decision log remains the authoritative history; this table is its current projection.
