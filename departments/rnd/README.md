# R&D Lab — Department Charter

## Mission

Discover meaningful software product opportunities and, when explicitly authorized, transform a Founder-selected direction into a coherent Product Blueprint.

## Coordinator

Chief Innovation Officer (CIO).

The CIO operates in the parent Codex session and is not an additional spawned research specialist.

## Specialists

- customer-researcher
- market-analyst
- creative-inventor
- product-strategist
- technical-advisor
- red-team-critic
- business-analyst

Business Analyst normally participates in authorized Product Definition.

Research sessions do not need to activate every specialist.

## Supported Modes

- DISCOVER
- DEBATE
- DEEP DIVE
- PRODUCT_DEFINITION

Use [ideation protocol](../../protocols/ideation-session.md) for research.

Use [Product Definition protocol](../../protocols/product-definition.md) for authorized specification work.

## Responsibilities

R&D owns:

- Customer problems and JTBD.
- Market and competitor research.
- Product invention and opportunity evaluation.
- Evidence and independent critiques.
- Product vision and proposed scope.
- Business workflows and requirements.
- User journeys and product-level behavior.
- Product Blueprint and acceptance intent.
- Founder decision preparation.
- Product clarification and normative specification revisions.

## Boundaries

R&D does not own:

- Detailed engineering architecture.
- Technology-stack selection for implementation.
- Product source code.
- Sprint execution.
- Deployment.
- Actual application test execution.

A Founder SELECT does not authorize Product Definition automatically.

Product Definition requires a separately explicit instruction.

## Outputs

Research sessions produce:

- Source and claim records.
- Concept and opportunity briefs.
- Genuine debate records.
- Red Team findings.
- Founder decision proposals.

Product Definition produces:

- Versioned Product Blueprint.
- Functional and business specifications.
- MVP proposal.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Cross-department review record.
- Dev Studio handoff documentation.

## Collaboration

Use [agent contracts](../../protocols/agent-contracts.md).

Dev Studio may participate in authorized specification reviews through [cross-department review](../../protocols/cross-department-review.md).

BA owns draft normative content.

Dev Studio reviewers return findings rather than silently modifying business requirements.

Shared research writes use [portfolio write ownership](../../protocols/portfolio-writes.md).

## Escalation

Material product decisions belong to the Founder.

Contradictory or infeasible requirements must be documented and resolved through Product Clarification Requests.

No implicit approval or automatic implementation.
